Frequently Asked Questions

Q: How does Offstage keep my notes hidden when I share my screen?

Offstage asks macOS to leave the notes window out of screen captures. Video-call and recording apps get your screen from macOS, so they receive your screen without the notes, while you still see them. You can share your entire screen and the notes stay private.

Q: How can I check that my notes are hidden?

Click the Offstage icon in the menu bar and choose Test: Hidden from Screen Share. The test takes a real screen capture on your Mac and tells you whether the notes appeared in it. macOS asks for Screen Recording permission the first time; only this test needs it. Before an important session, we also recommend a short test call where you watch your shared screen from another device.

Q: My notes appeared in a shared screen. What should I do?

Update your video-call app to its latest version and run the test above. Some video-call apps have an advanced screen-capture option in their screen-sharing settings. If your notes appear, turn on the option that uses the system's window filtering. If the test says "Visible", share only the window you are presenting instead of the entire screen, and email us your macOS version so we can look into it.

Q: How do I write notes with several cards?

Press Control-Option-E to edit your notes. Put a line with three dashes (---) between cards, one card per slide or topic. Lines starting with # become headings and lines starting with - become bullet points. You can also import a text or Markdown file from the menu.

Q: What are the keyboard shortcuts?

They work while any app is in front: Control-Option-Right Arrow (next card), Control-Option-Left Arrow (previous card), Control-Option-H (show or hide notes), Control-Option-E (edit), Control-Option-T (start or pause the timer), Control-Option-R (reset the timer), Control-Option-S (auto-scroll), Control-Option-Plus and Control-Option-Minus (text size), and Control-Option-C (click-through). The full list is in the menu under Keyboard Shortcuts.

Q: Can I keep the notes near my camera?

Yes. Choose Move Under Camera from the menu to place the notes just below the menu bar, close to the camera, so you keep eye contact while reading.

Q: The app says an update is required. What do I do?

Click Update to open the download page, install the latest version, and open Offstage again. Your notes are saved on your Mac and are kept when you update. The update message never interrupts a presentation: it waits until you have finished.

Q: How do I turn off usage statistics?

Click the Offstage icon in the menu bar and untick Share Anonymous Usage Stats. Your notes are never sent, whatever this setting is. See our Privacy Policy for details.

Q: How do I report a bug?

Please email us at shebinkoshyios@gmail.com with a description of the problem, your Mac model, your macOS version and the video-call app you were using. Screenshots are helpful!
