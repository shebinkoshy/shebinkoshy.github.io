Privacy Policy for Offstage

Last updated: November 7, 2026

Offstage is a macOS app that shows your speaker notes on your own screen while keeping them out of screen sharing and screen recordings. This policy explains what information the app handles, what leaves your Mac, and the choices you have.

Summary

  • Your notes never leave your Mac. We never receive, read or store the text of your notes or the names of your files.
  • Offstage has no accounts, no advertising and no tracking across apps or websites. We do not sell or share your information.
  • With your permission (on by default, and you can turn it off at any time), Offstage sends anonymous usage statistics and crash reports to our own server so we can fix problems and improve the app.
  • Each time it starts, Offstage asks our server whether an update is required. Nothing about that request is stored.
  • If you contact us through Contact Support, we keep your messages, and your email address if you choose to give it, so we can answer you.

Who We Are

Offstage is developed by Shebin Koshy (Zingsentek), an individual developer based in Bengaluru, India. For any question about this policy or your information, contact shebinkoshyios@gmail.com.

Your Notes and Files

Your notes and app settings are saved only on your Mac, in the app's own storage folder. When you import a text or Markdown file, it is read on your Mac only. None of this is uploaded to us or to anyone else.

Screen Recording Permission

Offstage includes an optional test that checks your notes are hidden from screen sharing. Only this test asks for macOS Screen Recording permission. The test captures one image of your screen, checks it on your Mac straight away, and then discards it. The image is never saved or sent anywhere. Only the result (hidden, visible or inconclusive) may be included in the usage statistics described below.

Anonymous Usage Statistics

If "Share Anonymous Usage Stats" is turned on in the Offstage menu (it is on by default), the app sends the following to our analytics server:

  • App events: app installed, updated or opened; a presentation took place (its length in minutes, how many cards were shown, whether the timer was used); notes were edited (the number of cards and a size range such as "501-2k characters", never the text); which features were used (for example the timer, auto-scroll or text size); the result of the screen-sharing test; keyboard shortcuts that another app already uses; and update prompts shown or clicked.
  • Device and app information: macOS version, Mac model, screen size, language and region settings, time zone, and the app's version.
  • A random installation ID created by the app. It lets us count installations and sessions without knowing who you are. It is not linked to your name, email, Apple ID or any other identifier.
  • Approximate location: our server works out the country, region, city, postal code and approximate coordinates (the centre of the city or postal area, rounded to about 1 km) from the network address of each upload. Your IP address itself is never stored. We never use GPS or the macOS location service.

We use this information only to understand how Offstage is used, to decide what to improve, and to check that features work on every macOS version.

To turn it off: click the Offstage icon in the menu bar and untick Share Anonymous Usage Stats. Usage statistics and crash reports then stop immediately, and anything still waiting to be sent is deleted from your Mac. The setting is remembered.

Crash and Error Reports

While usage statistics are on, Offstage also reports crashes and errors. A report contains technical details only: the type of error, the place in the app's code where it happened, recent app events (the same kinds of events listed above), the Mac model, macOS version, available memory, battery level and low-power mode, and whether the app was in use. A report never contains your notes.

Support Chat

Choosing Contact Support in the Offstage menu opens our support chat, which runs on our own server. If you use it, we store:

  • the messages you write and our replies;
  • your email address and name, only if you choose to enter them, so we can reply to you later;
  • the app's version, the platform (macOS), your language setting and the web engine used to show the chat;
  • an approximate location (country, region, city, time zone and approximate coordinates) worked out from your network address when you write. Your IP address itself is never stored.

A random token is kept in the chat window's storage on your Mac, so you see the same conversation when you come back. We use this information only to answer and resolve your request and to improve our support. Unlike the usage statistics, a conversation is linked to the email address you give us, so you can ask us to show or delete it at any time. Do not share passwords or other sensitive information in the chat. If the chat cannot be reached, the app offers email instead. Your email program then sends us only what you write.

Update Check

When Offstage starts, and at most once an hour while it runs, it asks our configuration server whether a newer version is required. The request contains the app's version, the platform ("macos"), your language setting and a separate random installation ID. The server uses your network address only briefly, to apply rate limits and to tell which country the request comes from. It does not store the request, the ID or your address. This check happens even when usage statistics are off, because it keeps the app working correctly. It sends nothing about you or your notes.

What We Do Not Do

  • We do not collect your contacts or the contents of your notes or screen. We only have your name or email address if you give them to us in the support chat or by email.
  • We do not show ads or use advertising identifiers.
  • We do not track you across other companies' apps or websites.
  • We do not sell, rent or share your information with third parties for their own purposes.

Where Data Is Stored and Who Handles It

Usage statistics and crash reports are stored on servers that we operate ourselves, located in India. We do not use third-party analytics services. Network traffic to our servers passes through Cloudflare, which provides security and delivery services and processes network data (such as IP addresses) on our behalf. See the Cloudflare Privacy Policy. If you download Offstage from the Mac App Store, Apple handles the download, any purchases, and App Store analytics under Apple's Privacy Policy.

If you are outside India, your information is transferred to India. We protect it as described in this policy wherever it is processed.

How Long We Keep Data

We keep usage statistics and crash reports only as long as they help us maintain and improve Offstage, and we delete them when they are no longer needed for that purpose. We keep support conversations for as long as needed to help you and to handle any follow-up, and we delete them on request. Data saved on your Mac stays there until you delete it or remove the app.

Legal Basis (EEA, UK and Switzerland)

We process usage statistics and crash reports on the basis of our legitimate interest in keeping Offstage reliable and improving it (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR). We keep this data anonymous by design, and you can switch it off at any time from the app's menu. We process the update check because it is necessary to provide a working, supported app. We process support conversations to respond to your request (Article 6(1)(b) GDPR) and on the basis of our legitimate interest in providing good support.

Your Rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR, UK GDPR, California's CCPA/CPRA, Brazil's LGPD, Singapore's PDPA or India's DPDP Act), you may have the right to access, correct or delete your information, to object to or restrict its use, and to complain to your local data protection authority. You can stop all collection yourself with the Share Anonymous Usage Stats setting.

Support conversations are linked to the email address you gave us, so we can find, export or delete them when you ask. Usage statistics and crash reports are linked only to a random installation ID, not to your name or email. We therefore cannot identify which of those records are yours from your name or email address alone. If you would like us to act on a request, email shebinkoshyios@gmail.com and we will do our best to help. California residents: we do not sell or "share" personal information as those terms are defined in the CCPA/CPRA.

Children

Offstage is a general audience productivity app. It is not directed at children under 13 (or under 16 in the EEA), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Security

All data sent by Offstage travels over encrypted HTTPS connections. Our servers store only the information described above, and access to them is restricted.

Changes to This Policy

If we change how Offstage handles information, we will update this page and the "Last updated" date. Significant changes will also be noted in the app's release notes.
